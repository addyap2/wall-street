# Wall Street promo: three storylines

Source: the site's own footage (`videos/`, `images/live.mp4`), photos, logo, and copy.
Brand: periwinkle #7B93D4, coral #E85466, cream #F4ECDD, ink blue #223163; Anton / Oswald / Kaushan Script.
Format assumption: 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) for Reels/TikTok/Stories, since nearly all footage is shot vertical; 25–35 s; music + SFX, no voiceover. A 16:9 cut can follow.

## A. « Une soirée au Wall Street » (recommended: a stranger follows a night out)
Device: a moment in someone's day · Form: an object-to-object chain.
A clock-style time stamp drives one evening from 18h to late. Every cut is caused by the frame before it: the pint being poured tilts into the wok's toss, the plate lifts into the crowd at the screen, the match's roar cuts into the live band, the band's light into the lit façade sign.
- First 2 s: frame 0 is a Paulaner glass under the tap, beer rising; "18h00" in Anton slams in coral, with "Afterwork" in Kaushan Script under it.
- Beats: 18h afterwork pint → 19h30 the kitchen (wok, entrecôte, tiramisu) → 20h45 kick-off on the big screen → 22h live music → façade at night → logo end card: "36 rue de l'Intendance · Fréjus" + "Réservez votre table"
- Look: the footage full-bleed; a periwinkle band that wipes the cuts and carries the time stamp; cream type, with coral rationed to the hour.
- Sound: an upbeat funk/pub-rock bed; the tap's pour, sizzle, crowd roar and guitar stab hit on the matching cuts.

## B. « Good food • Good drinks • Good people »
Device: type carries the argument · Form: cut on the beat.
The site's own three-part tagline is the spine. Giant Anton words fill the frame in periwinkle and coral blocks, and the footage plays *inside* the letters, then bursts out full-frame. Three chapters, one per word.
- First 2 s: "GOOD" fills the frame; the wok footage flames inside the letters, then "FOOD" punches in on the downbeat.
- Beats: GOOD FOOD (wok, plates, dessert) → GOOD DRINKS (Delirium tap, pints, cocktail) → GOOD PEOPLE (crowd, match, live) → "Good Times" in script → logo end card
- Look: flat brand-colour fields, hard cuts every half beat, footage masked in type; the marquee ticker from the site as a moving strip.
- Sound: a punchy 120 bpm track with every cut on the beat.

## C. « Passez la porte » (the bold one)
Device: the result first · Form: one continuous take / endless zoom.
We start outside on the glowing sign at night, and the camera dives through the door. Each dive lands on a detail (a beer tap, a TV screen, a plate) that becomes the next room, as if one unbroken walk through the pub.
- First 2 s: the night façade sign "Live Music & Sports" flickers on, then a push straight into the door.
- Beats: façade → push through into the bar (tour footage) → dive into the Delirium tap → into the kitchen → into the TV (match) → into the stage light (live) → pull all the way back out to the façade, with the logo landing on the sign → end card
- Look: zoom-through transitions with motion blur, minimal type (one word per room in Oswald caps), and a warm night grade.
- Sound: an atmospheric build, the room tone changing at each door (crowd murmur → sizzle → roar → band), resolving on the end card.
